## 💼 Featured Project

### [BizKipe](https://bizkipev11.vercel.app/)

> Get paid faster. Run your business from your phone.

BizKipe is a free business tool built for small businesses and freelancers in Kenya. It helps users create invoices, quotations, track payments, manage expenses, and send reminders without the headache.

#### Key Features
- 🧾 Create invoices and quotations in minutes
- 🎨 Add your logo and brand colors
- 🔁 Turn quotations into invoices in one tap
- 💰 Track payments and monitor who owes you
- 📲 Send WhatsApp reminders to defaulters
- 📊 View income, expense, and business reports
- 🖨️ Print reports and summaries
- 📱 Mobile-friendly and easy to use

**Live demo:** [bizkipev11.vercel.app](https://bizkipev11.vercel.app/)  
**Type:** Free business tool for SMEs and freelancers  
**Ready for:** M-Pesa workflows and local business operations
